\subsection{Analysis and Design}

\subsubsection{Database Schema}

Sprint 3 introduces three new entity groups: \texttt{reviews} (with \texttt{rating}, \texttt{ai\_summary}, and \texttt{flagged\_for\_moderation} fields) and \texttt{asset\_likes}; \texttt{notifications} (with \texttt{type}, \texttt{is\_read}, and \texttt{related\_id} for deep-linking); and \texttt{subscription\_plans} with \texttt{user\_subscriptions}.

Reviews are restricted to users who own the asset. The admin moderation panel provides a paginated queue of pending submissions — the admin can preview, approve (setting status to \texttt{approved}), or reject with a written reason forwarded to the seller via notification. All actions are recorded in the audit log.

\subsection{Sprint Review --- Deliverables}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Review and rating system with ownership enforcement and automatic rating recalculation.
  \item Asset like/favorite system with per-asset counter.
  \item In-app notification center with unread badge and mark-as-read functionality.
  \item Admin moderation panel: approve or reject with written reason; full audit trail.
  \item Platform analytics dashboard (total sales, top assets, revenue overview).
  \item Three-tier subscription system (Free, Subscriber, Seller) with plan management UI.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Conclusion}
Sprint 3 enriched the platform with trust-building and governance features. The combination of user reviews, admin moderation, and subscription tiers creates a sustainable ecosystem.
