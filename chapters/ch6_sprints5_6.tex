\section*{Introduction}
This chapter presents the two sprints that extend IGS Marketplace into a broader studio
ecosystem. Sprint 5 introduces the IGS Workspace, a private internal application for team,
project, task, and chat management. Sprint 6 integrates AI services for recommendations,
review analysis, and chatbot assistance.

\subsection{Analysis and Design}

The IGS Workspace is implemented as a separate React + Vite application under
\texttt{IGS\_Workspace}. It has its own layout, sidebar, top bar, pages, services, and
contexts, but consumes the same backend and identity layer as the marketplace. This design
preserves a clear separation between public marketplace workflows and internal studio
workflows.

Workspace data is stored in dedicated tables: \texttt{projects}, \texttt{project\_members},
\texttt{project\_documents}, \texttt{workspace\_tasks}, \texttt{chat\_channels},
\texttt{chat\_messages}, and related chat tables.

\subsection{Implementation}

The backend workspace routes are grouped in \texttt{workspace.js}. They expose endpoints for
team management, dashboard statistics, tasks, projects, project members, project documents,
and project-specific tasks. Real-time chat is implemented through \texttt{chat.js},
\texttt{chatController.js}, \texttt{chatRepository.js}, and Socket.io.

The frontend workspace includes pages such as Dashboard, Projects, Project Detail, Tasks,
Team, Chat, Assets, Users, Roles, Orders, Calendar, Reports, Performance, and Settings.
Shared services such as \texttt{workspaceService.js} and \texttt{api.js} isolate backend
communication.

\subsection{Sprint Review}

Sprint 5 delivered the internal application needed by the studio. Internal users could
manage projects, tasks, teams, and chat in a single environment while keeping access
restricted to authorized roles.

\subsection{Analysis and Design}

The AI layer is integrated as a service layer rather than being embedded directly into
controllers. This keeps the platform flexible: the provider or model can evolve without
rewriting marketplace routes. User events such as views, likes, downloads, and purchases are
stored in \texttt{user\_events} and used by the recommendation service.

\subsection{Implementation}

AI logic is grouped in \texttt{aiService.js}, \texttt{intentService.js}, and
\texttt{recommendationService.js}. The chatbot is exposed through \texttt{bot.js} and
\texttt{botController.js}. Review analysis is connected to the review workflow, while
recommendations are generated from stored user interactions and asset metadata.

On the frontend, \texttt{BotWidget.jsx} provides a floating assistant interface and
marketplace pages can consume recommendation results without knowing the implementation
details of the AI provider.

\subsection{Sprint Review}

Sprint 6 added intelligent assistance to the marketplace. The system could support review
moderation, recommend relevant assets, and answer user questions through a conversational
interface.

\section*{Conclusion}
Sprints 5 and 6 expanded IGS Marketplace beyond commercial workflows. The platform gained an
internal studio workspace and AI-assisted features that improve collaboration, discovery, and
moderation.
