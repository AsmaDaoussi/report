\chapter{Sprints 5--6: Advanced Features}

\section*{Introduction}
Chapter 6 covers Sprints 5 and 6, which elevate IGS Marketplace from a standalone e-commerce platform to a comprehensive ecosystem. Sprint 5 delivers the IGS Workspace — a dedicated internal collaboration application for the studio team, featuring real-time chat, task management, and project tracking. Sprint 6 integrates Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence capabilities: trained open-source models for intent classification and sentiment analysis, and a Mistral-7B-powered marketplace assistant chatbot.
