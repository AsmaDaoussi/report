\chapter*{General Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{General Introduction}

The global digital content economy is undergoing remarkable expansion. The worldwide 3D model marketplace market was valued at \textbf{USD~1.92 billion} in 2023 and is projected to reach \textbf{USD~4.86 billion by 2031}, growing at a compound annual growth rate of 11.2\%. This growth is driven by the booming video game industry, architectural visualization, and film production, accelerated by the widespread adoption of real-time rendering engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine. Studios worldwide increasingly trade and outsource digital asset creation, giving rise to a thriving ecosystem of online marketplaces that serve creators and buyers of 3D models, textures, animations, shaders, and game-ready content.

Despite this global trend, \textbf{Tunisia had no dedicated 3D digital asset marketplace} before this project. Tunisian studios and developers were entirely dependent on foreign platforms — Unity Asset Store, Sketchfab, Fab, CGTrader — which impose dollar-denominated pricing, foreign payment gateways, and no customization. \textbf{Inherited Games Studio (IGS)}, a Tunisian video game development company, identified this gap and commissioned the development of \textbf{IGS Marketplace}, the first dedicated 3D digital asset marketplace designed and built in Tunisia. The platform must simultaneously serve external creators and buyers needing a seamless marketplace experience, internal employees and interns requiring role-based access to studio tools, and administrators demanding full control over content, users, and platform analytics. The result is a complete full-stack web platform composed of two interconnected applications: \textbf{IGS Marketplace}, the public-facing storefront with an interactive 3D viewer, Stripe payment, AI services, and subscription plans; and \textbf{IGS Workspace}, a companion internal dashboard for team management, task tracking, and real-time collaboration — both sharing the same Express.js backend, PostgreSQL database, and Firebase authentication.

This report is organized following the \textbf{Scrum} agile methodology, with each chapter covering a phase or pair of sprints. \textbf{Chapter 1} establishes the general project context, the comparative study of existing solutions, the chosen methodology, and the technology stack. \textbf{Chapter 2} details the requirements analysis, actors, functional and non-functional requirements, and global system architecture. \textbf{Chapter 3} covers Sprint 0 — the research, planning, and architectural decisions made before any code was written. \textbf{Chapters 4 and 5} document Sprints 1 through 4, delivering the core platform: authentication, RBAC, asset catalog, payment, reviews, and administration. \textbf{Chapters 6 and 7} cover Sprints 5 through 8, delivering the IGS Workspace, AI integration, the recommendation engine, and the final UI overhaul. \textbf{Chapter 8} presents the CI/CD pipeline, Docker containerization, and production deployment strategy.
