\section*{Introduction}
This chapter describes the development of the IGS public marketplace. It covers the
research and architectural decisions that prepared the project, the security and access
foundation, the catalog and browsing experience, the seller moderation workflow, and the
role-specific marketplace features that make the platform operational.

\section{Sprint 0: Research, Planning \& Architecture}

\subsection{Sprint Backlog}

\begin{sprintbox}{Sprint 0 -- Marketplace Preparation}
\textbf{Goal:} establish the technical strategy, choose the technology stack, and design the
main architecture before implementing the marketplace.
\end{sprintbox}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Sprint 0 Backlog}
\begin{tabular}{L{1.5cm} L{4.5cm} L{6cm}}

\midrule

T-03 & Select architecture, frameworks, and deployment strategy. & Minimize technical risk. \\

T-05 & Plan the sprint structure for marketplace and workspace delivery. & Align implementation with academic reporting. \\

The foundational architecture relies on a three-tier separation: two React + Vite SPAs
(public marketplace and internal workspace), a Node.js/Express backend API, and a
PostgreSQL database with Supabase storage for binary assets. This clear separation allows
each layer to evolve independently while preserving a single shared identity and access
model.

\section{Sprint 1: Authentication, Users and RBAC}

\subsection{Sprint Backlog}

\begin{sprintbox}{Sprint 1 -- Secure Marketplace Access}
\textbf{Goal:} establish a secure authentication and authorization layer for the marketplace.
\end{sprintbox}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Sprint 1 Backlog}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{C{1.1cm} X C{2cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{User Story} & \textbf{Priority} \\
\midrule
US-01 & As a visitor, I can create an account and sign in securely. & High \\
US-02 & As a user, I can access my profile and update my information. & High \\
US-03 & As an administrator, I can assign roles and permissions. & High \\
US-04 & As the system, I can protect API routes and frontend pages. & High \\
US-05 & As an administrator, I can trace sensitive access events. & Medium \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Authentication uses Supabase Auth to issue JWTs. The backend validates the token,
populates \texttt{req.user} with roles and permissions, and protects sensitive routes with
centralized middleware. This ensures that marketplace pages and admin actions only become
available to the appropriate user groups.

\section{Sprint 3: Upload, Moderation \& Trust}

\subsection{Sprint Backlog}

\begin{sprintbox}{Sprint 3 -- Seller and Moderation Workflow}
\textbf{Goal:} unlock seller contributions while ensuring marketplace quality through moderation.
\end{sprintbox}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Sprint 3 Backlog}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{C{1.1cm} X C{2cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{User Story} & \textbf{Priority} \\
\midrule
US-12 & As a seller, I can upload an asset with files, thumbnail, tags, metadata, and price. & High \\
US-13 & As an admin, I can approve or reject submitted assets. & High \\
US-14 & As a seller, I can request edits for approved assets. & Medium \\
US-15 & As a buyer, I can rate and review purchased assets. & Medium \\
US-16 & As a user, I can receive notifications about moderation and order events. & Medium \\
US-17 & As a user, I can consult and subscribe to available plans. & Medium \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

A moderated upload pipeline prevents unvetted content from appearing in the catalog.
Assets are created with \texttt{pending} status, and authorization rules ensure that only
internal reviewers can approve or reject submissions. Buyers may leave ratings and reviews
only after purchase, supporting a trustable marketplace reputation system.

\subsection{Sprint Backlog}

\section*{Conclusion}
Chapter 3 consolidated the marketplace delivery strategy from planning to launch. The
development path progressed from architectural design to secure access, public catalog
features, moderated seller uploads, and role-based workflows, establishing a complete
marketplace experience.
