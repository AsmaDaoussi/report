\chapter{Sprints 1--2: Core Platform}

\section*{Introduction}
Chapter 4 covers the first two development sprints, which together deliver the entire core technical infrastructure of IGS Marketplace. Sprint 1 establishes the backend foundation — authentication, Role-Based Access Control, and the PostgreSQL database schema. Sprint 2 builds directly upon it to deliver the end-user-facing marketplace: the asset catalog, interactive 3D viewer, persistent shopping cart, and Stripe payment integration.
