\chapter{Sprints 3--4: Marketplace Experience}

\section*{Introduction}
Chapter 5 covers Sprints 3 and 4, which add the trust and governance layer of the marketplace and extend it with role-specific advanced features. Sprint 3 introduces the review and rating system, the notification center, admin content moderation tools, and subscription plans. Sprint 4 delivers the intern asset workflow, peer-to-peer trading, seller analytics, and community groups — making the RBAC system operationally meaningful for every user segment.
